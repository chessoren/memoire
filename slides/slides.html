How it works

Gift it once. Hear her every week.

1 · Gift it

Her child writes a 4-minute portrait: places, work, topics to avoid, call time.

2 · Louise calls

Every Sunday, an AI biographer phones her and asks about her life.

3 · The family listens

That night: her real voice, the transcript in sync, a chapter for the book.

4 · Ask, she answers

“How did Grandma meet Grandpa?” The answer is her, saying it.

4
The biographer

Louise listens like a person, and remembers like a notebook.

“That is such a beautiful memory, Jeanne. What was the name of the street where the bakery was located?”
  • Real-time voice with Gemini Live (gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking) over a raw WebSocket. No SDK.
  • Patient turn-taking for elderly speakers who pause mid-sentence: low end-of-speech sensitivity, 1.6 s of silence.
  • Oral-history method: concrete before abstract, follow-up on details, always ask names, thread between calls, never raise the topics the family flagged.
  • Honest by design: says she's an AI, asks consent to record, is never a companion.

5
After the call

A chapter in her own words. Nothing added.

When the call ends, Gemini 3.8 Flash (high thinking, JSON schema) turns the transcript into a chapter. It cleans the hesitations, keeps her expressions, and is forbidden to add a fact, a name or a date.

  • Her voice is recorded, not the AI's. The archive keeps only the storyteller's side, with a timestamp for every turn.
  • People, places and years go into a living dossier Louise reads before the next call.
  • Never lose a recording. If Gemini is overloaded, it falls back to lighter models, and if all fail, the voice is saved and the chapter waits.

The voice archive

Ask anything. Her real voice answers.

A grandson types “How did Grandma meet Grandpa?” Mémoire finds the passage where she tells it, plays exactly those seconds, and highlights each sentence as she says it.

  • Searched on the iPhone with Apple's NaturalLanguage sentence embeddings over timestamped segments. Her stories don't leave the family to answer a question.
  • It never generates an answer. Every result is a real excerpt of a real call.
  • Not there yet? One tap sends the question to Louise for next Sunday.
